# Code of Conduct

PTR contributors are expected to keep technical discussion professional, evidence-driven and respectful.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Technical disagreement, falsification of hypotheses and critical review are encouraged when focused on the work rather than the person.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior materially harms contributors or the project.
