# M011-routed-peft

## Hypothesis
routed LoRA experts (PERFT-R) on OLMoE beat a single LoRA and MixLoRA-style experts at equal trainable parameters, and emergent specialisation is at least as good as prescribed code/math/tool specialisation; emergent specialisation must be non-inferior to prescribed specialisation within 1 point, mean task accuracy being the macro average over domains, and every arm gets the same preregistered tuning budget; the PERFT-R arm used for the 1-point criteria is the emergent one, and LoRA rank is pinned per arm to match trainable parameters within 1%, not tuned; adapter topology is pinned per arm with explicit target tensors and shapes so trainable parameters match within 1% of a common target of 4456448 (PERFT-R arms: 8 parallel bottleneck PEFT experts at rank 8 with a router, base expert projections not adapted; MixLoRA: 9 LoRA experts at rank 4 as deltas on the fused expert gate-and-up and down projections with a top-2 router; plain LoRA: attention q, k, v, o at rank 17), computed from the shapes and recorded; the prescribed specialisation assigns experts 0 to 2 to code, 3 to 5 to math and 6 to 7 to tool, with routing masked by the task's domain label in training and evaluation (top-2 among the domain's experts) and the assignment digest recorded, while the emergent arm routes unmasked. Hyperparameters are selected once, on the tuning seed (tuning_seed = 1001, a pilot seed that is never one of the confirmatory seeds) with shortened trials of tuning_steps = 500 (floor(0.25 * training_steps_per_arm = 2000), minimum 1), then frozen for every seed's full-budget run; tuning_seed, tuning_steps and the selected hyperparameters are recorded with the procedure parameters.

## Primary metrics
task_accuracy_per_domain, expert_specialisation_index, trainable_params, latency_per_token, selection_digest_mismatches, procedure_mismatches, tuning_trials_used, tuning_budget_mismatches, perft_vs_lora_gain_points, perft_vs_mixlora_gain_points, training_data_outside_training_partition_count, training_flops, training_flops_mismatch_seeds, validation_overlap_count, validation_set_digest, model_pin_mismatches, trainable_params_by_arm, validation_set_digest_by_corpus, validation_overlap_count_by_corpus, tuning_trial_set_digest, expert_domain_assignment_digest

## Baseline
plain (single) LoRA at equal trainable parameters, equal training steps and total training FLOPs including routing within 5%; MixLoRA-style top-k LoRA experts at equal trainable parameters, equal training steps and total training FLOPs including routing within 5% (there is no frozen-base arm)

## Falsification
The run is invalid (vacuous) and counts as falsified if any coverage prerequisite is unmet: trainable_params differ by more than the pinned relative tolerance (1%) between arms, or arms trained with different budgets; task_accuracy_per_domain missing for any pinned domain (code, math, tool) in any arm or seed; selection_digest_mismatches > 0 (the corpus selection digest recorded in run.json differs from the pinned digest) or procedure_mismatches > 0 (a pinned run-procedure parameter recorded per arm or cell in run.json differs from the pinned value; equality across arms is also compared against the pinned value); tuning_budget_mismatches > 0 (tuning_trials_used per arm differs from the pinned 8); training_data_outside_training_partition_count > 0 (training data must come only from the training partition); training_flops_mismatch_seeds > 0 (total training FLOPs including routing, measured per arm and seed, differ between arms by more than flops_relative_tolerance = 0.05 in any seed); validation_overlap_count > 0 for any corpus (validation overlapping training, gate or held-out evaluation, counted per corpus), or validation_set_digest is not recorded; model_pin_mismatches > 0 (the base checkpoint (repo and revision), the tokenizer (repo and revision) or the initial MoE topology recorded in run.json differs from the pin in config.toml, or the tiny-model config digest differs); tuning_search_spec recorded in run.json differs from the pinned search space, trial budget, sampler, selection seed, objective or tie-break (procedure_mismatches); validation_set_digest or validation_overlap_count is missing for any of the validation corpora gsm8k_train, humaneval or bfcl_simple (each corpus has its own digest and overlap counters); tuning_trials_used != trial_budget (8) for any tuned arm, or tuning_trial_set_digest is not recorded in run.json (procedure_mismatches); expert_domain_assignment_digest is not recorded in run.json or differs from the pinned assignment. Falsified if any of: the emergent PERFT-R arm's macro-average task accuracy is not better than the plain (single) LoRA's by at least 1 point (perft_vs_lora_margin_points = 1.0); the emergent PERFT-R arm's macro-average task accuracy is not better than MixLoRA-style experts' by at least 1 point (perft_vs_mixlora_margin_points = 1.0); prescribed specialisation beats emergent by more than 1 point (emergent specialisation must be non-inferior within 1 point); any one of the 5 listed seeds misses any criterion above (every seed must pass). Mean task accuracy is the macro average over the pinned domains; LoRA rank is fixed per arm from the trainable-parameter count before any run and is not tuned.

## Rule
Record matched baselines, hardware, seeds and negative results. Do not change success criteria after observing results.

## Implementation facts (not part of the hypothesis or the criteria)
- Entrypoint module: `training/src/ptr_training/moe/m011.py` (the manifest's `entrypoint` is still empty). Its pins are compared with this `config.toml` and `experiment.toml` by `training/tests/test_m011_*`, including every arm's `target_tensors` against the instantiated arm.
- `run.json` records the git commit and dirty flag, package versions, device and GPU facts, the HumanEval sandbox mode, the seeds, the corpus selection and validation digests, the measured `training_outside_training_partition_count` and the observed model topology; `metrics.json` records `task_accuracy_per_domain`, `latency_per_token` (definition chosen in code, not pinned here) and `training_flops`. `expert_specialisation_index` is not computed: this experiment pins no definition for it.
- `--tune` refuses a confirmatory seed or a seed below the pilot seed 1001.
- Memory, estimated by `python -m ptr_training.moe.memory --m011-arm <arm>` (not measured): about 17.7 GiB allocated at peak for the PERFT-R arms, 17.3 GiB for plain LoRA and 20.8 GiB for MixLoRA (its evaluation generation, about 1.2 GiB below the budget) at the entrypoint defaults (bf16, micro-batch 4 x 1024 tokens, gradient checkpointing, evaluation batch 16 with prompts assumed up to 1024 tokens), within one 24 GiB RTX 3090.
- Nothing has run on the real model, the real corpora or a GPU.
