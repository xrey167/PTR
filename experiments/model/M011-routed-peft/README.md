# M011-routed-peft

## Hypothesis
routed LoRA experts (PERFT-R) on OLMoE beat a single LoRA and MixLoRA-style experts at equal trainable parameters, and emergent specialisation is at least as good as prescribed code/math/tool specialisation; emergent specialisation must be non-inferior to prescribed specialisation within 1 point, mean task accuracy being the macro average over domains, and every arm gets the same preregistered tuning budget; the PERFT-R arm used for the 1-point criteria is the emergent one, and LoRA rank is pinned per arm to match trainable parameters within 1%, not tuned; adapter topology is pinned per arm with explicit target tensors and shapes so trainable parameters match within 1% of a common target of 4456448 (PERFT-R arms: 8 parallel bottleneck PEFT experts at rank 8 with a router, base expert projections not adapted; MixLoRA: 9 LoRA experts at rank 4 as deltas on the fused expert gate-and-up and down projections with a top-2 router; plain LoRA: attention q, k, v, o at rank 17), computed from the shapes and recorded

## Primary metrics
task_accuracy_per_domain, expert_specialisation_index, trainable_params, latency_per_token, selection_digest_mismatches, procedure_mismatches, tuning_trials_used, tuning_budget_mismatches, perft_vs_lora_gain_points, perft_vs_mixlora_gain_points, training_data_outside_training_partition_count, training_flops, training_flops_mismatch_seeds, validation_overlap_count, validation_set_digest, model_pin_mismatches, trainable_params_by_arm, validation_set_digest_by_corpus, validation_overlap_count_by_corpus, tuning_trial_set_digest

## Baseline
plain (single) LoRA at equal trainable parameters, equal training steps and total training FLOPs including routing within 5%; MixLoRA-style top-k LoRA experts at equal trainable parameters, equal training steps and total training FLOPs including routing within 5% (there is no frozen-base arm)

## Falsification
The run is invalid (vacuous) and counts as falsified if any coverage prerequisite is unmet: trainable_params differ by more than the pinned relative tolerance (1%) between arms, or arms trained with different budgets; task_accuracy_per_domain missing for any pinned domain (code, math, tool) in any arm or seed; selection_digest_mismatches > 0 (the corpus selection digest recorded in run.json differs from the pinned digest) or procedure_mismatches > 0 (a pinned run-procedure parameter recorded per arm or cell in run.json differs from the pinned value; equality across arms is also compared against the pinned value); tuning_budget_mismatches > 0 (tuning_trials_used per arm differs from the pinned 8); training_data_outside_training_partition_count > 0 (training data must come only from the training partition); training_flops_mismatch_seeds > 0 (total training FLOPs including routing, measured per arm and seed, differ between arms by more than flops_relative_tolerance = 0.05 in any seed); validation_overlap_count > 0 for any corpus (validation overlapping training, gate or held-out evaluation, counted per corpus), or validation_set_digest is not recorded; model_pin_mismatches > 0 (the base checkpoint (repo and revision), the tokenizer (repo and revision) or the initial MoE topology recorded in run.json differs from the pin in config.toml, or the tiny-model config digest differs); tuning_search_spec recorded in run.json differs from the pinned search space, trial budget, sampler, selection seed, objective or tie-break (procedure_mismatches); validation_set_digest or validation_overlap_count is missing for any of the validation corpora gsm8k_train, humaneval or bfcl_simple (each corpus has its own digest and overlap counters); tuning_trials_used != trial_budget (8) for any tuned arm, or tuning_trial_set_digest is not recorded in run.json (procedure_mismatches). Falsified if any of: the emergent PERFT-R arm's macro-average task accuracy is not better than the plain (single) LoRA's by at least 1 point (perft_vs_lora_margin_points = 1.0); the emergent PERFT-R arm's macro-average task accuracy is not better than MixLoRA-style experts' by at least 1 point (perft_vs_mixlora_margin_points = 1.0); prescribed specialisation beats emergent by more than 1 point (emergent specialisation must be non-inferior within 1 point); any one of the 5 listed seeds misses any criterion above (every seed must pass). Mean task accuracy is the macro average over the pinned domains; LoRA rank is fixed per arm from the trainable-parameter count before any run and is not tuned.

## Rule
Record matched baselines, hardware, seeds and negative results. Do not change success criteria after observing results.
