# M015-rlvr-vs-distill-passk

## Hypothesis
on the pod-MoE, RLVR raises pass@1 but not pass@256 relative to the base, while distillation from a stronger teacher raises large-k pass@k; pass@k (k up to 256) uses the unbiased estimator with 256 samples per problem on the full GSM8K test split (1319 problems), the teacher is Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B at a pinned revision, distillation compute is matched to RLVR training FLOPs, and the criteria are judged on paired bootstrap 95% confidence intervals over problems; the student base model is allenai/OLMoE-1B-7B-0125 at revision 9b0c1aa87e34a20052389dce1f0cf01da783f654, recorded in run.json, and every training arm's data (RLVR prompts and distillation data) is disjoint from the GSM8K test split; the teacher distillation protocol is pinned: 4 samples per prompt at temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95, top_k disabled, max_new_tokens 512 and a pinned teacher_seed, filtered to verifier-passing samples (exact final-answer match), the lowest passing sample index per prompt is selected, prompts with no passing sample are dropped and counted, and the distillation_set_digest is recorded; the RLVR and distillation prompt set is the GSM8K train items with position mod 10 != 9, the complement of the tuning-validation partition (mod 10 == 9).

## Primary metrics
pass_at_1, pass_at_k_curve, samples_per_problem, training_flops, pass_at_1_gain_ci_lower, pass_at_256_gain_ci_lower, pass_at_256_gain_ci_upper, training_test_overlap_count, training_set_digest, selection_digest_mismatches, procedure_mismatches, decoding_parameter_mismatches, tuning_trials_used, tuning_budget_mismatches, distill_prompts_dropped, distillation_set_digest, validation_overlap_count, validation_set_digest, model_pin_mismatches, tuning_trial_set_digest

## Baseline
base model; SFT-distilled control

## Falsification
The run is invalid (vacuous) and counts as falsified if any coverage prerequisite is unmet: samples_per_problem != 256 for any compared arm (base, RLVR, SFT-distilled); training_flops of the RLVR and distilled arms differ by more than compute_relative_tolerance (declared per arm); the pass@k curve is not recorded for every compared arm in every seed, or the pinned teacher and problem-set revisions are not recorded in run.json; the student base model identity and revision are not recorded in run.json, training_test_overlap_count > 0 for any training arm, or training_set_digest is missing for any arm (training data must be disjoint from the GSM8K test split); selection_digest_mismatches > 0 (the corpus selection digest recorded in run.json differs from the pinned digest) or procedure_mismatches > 0 (a pinned run-procedure parameter recorded per arm or cell in run.json differs from the pinned value; equality across arms is also compared against the pinned value); decoding_parameter_mismatches > 0 (temperature, top_p, top_k, max_new_tokens or n_samples recorded per arm differs from the pinned value); tuning_budget_mismatches > 0 (tuning_trials_used differs from the pinned 8 for any trained arm, RLVR or distilled; the untrained base arm has no tuning and must record 0); distillation_set_digest is not recorded in run.json (distill_prompts_dropped is recorded and reported); validation_overlap_count > 0 for any corpus (validation overlapping training, gate or held-out evaluation, counted per corpus), or validation_set_digest is not recorded; model_pin_mismatches > 0 (the base checkpoint (repo and revision), the tokenizer (repo and revision) or the initial MoE topology recorded in run.json differs from the pin in config.toml, or the tiny-model config digest differs); tuning_search_spec recorded in run.json differs from the pinned search space, trial budget, sampler, selection seed, objective or tie-break (procedure_mismatches); tuning_trials_used != trial_budget (8) for any tuned arm, or tuning_trial_set_digest is not recorded in run.json (procedure_mismatches). Falsified if any of: the lower bound of the paired bootstrap 95% CI of RLVR's pass@1 gain over the base is below 2 points; the lower bound pass_at_256_gain_ci_lower of the paired bootstrap 95% CI of RLVR's pass@256 gain over the base is above 2 points (gain shown); the lower bound of the paired bootstrap 95% CI of the distilled arm's pass@256 gain over the base is below 2 points; any one of the 5 listed seeds misses any criterion above (every seed must pass). Gains are absolute differences in points; the CIs use 10000 paired bootstrap resamples over problems with a pinned resampling seed. The no-pass@256-gain conclusion is confirmed only if the CI upper bound pass_at_256_gain_ci_upper of (RLVR pass@256 minus base pass@256) is at most 2 points; if the upper bound is above 2 and the lower bound is at most 2 the outcome is inconclusive (reported, not a confirmation, and not a pass of the claim).

## Rule
Record matched baselines, hardware, seeds and negative results. Do not change success criteria after observing results.
