# M016-approximate-chunk-reuse

## Hypothesis
on an N-layer decoder, position-independent chunk reuse at nonzero position shifts (pinned shifts 16, 64 and 256 tokens) with selective recompute (candidate recomputation sets k pinned as fractions [0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20] of chunk tokens, each rounded up to whole tokens; chunk_tokens = 64 gives k in {4, 7, 10, 13}) on a pinned 4-layer decoder (N >= 2) keeps top-1 next-token agreement >=99% while saving >=2x prefill; the 2x saving is measured only on the live-lineage edit path (upstream segments inserted, shifted or reordered with every recorded segment unchanged, nothing in the page's lineage revoked), where approximate upper-layer reuse with selective recompute is allowed and the result is tagged Approximate; upstream-revocation cases are safety checks only and are excluded from the speed metric; k is selected on a disjoint calibration split by a pinned rule (the smallest k in [4, 7, 10, 13] with top-1 agreement >= 99% at every shift) and then evaluated on a held-out split; recomputed tokens are the CacheBlend-style highest-deviation tokens in every layer >= 1; content edits (including deletions and redactions) supersede the old generation and are path (a), so the page is unreachable for every layer and freshly prefilled, while live-lineage edits mean insert, shift or reorder of unchanged segments; reuse remains correct under lagging or lost invalidation and restore or restart.

## Primary metrics
top1_agreement, kl_vs_full, prefill_speedup, recompute_fraction, secondary_index_lookups, nonaligned_insertion_cases, upstream_revocation_cases, revoked_upstream_page_reachable_count, layers_ge1_freshly_prefilled, live_lineage_edit_cases, test_model_layers, candidate_shift_results_cells, canary_logit_shift, canary_probes, canary_removing_edit_cases, reused_results, reused_results_tagged_approximate, approximate_tag_violations, revoked_page_layers_reused, post_revocation_secondary_lookups_matching_content, calibration_selected_k, heldout_top1_agreement, invalidation_suppressed_arms, restore_arms, post_event_lookups, stale_hits

## Baseline
full prefill

## Falsification
falsified if no single candidate k from the pinned set {ceil(f * chunk_tokens) : f in [0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20]} jointly meets BOTH top-1 agreement >= 99% AND prefill saving >= 2x at EVERY pinned nonzero position shift (16, 64, 256 per config.toml [pinned_thresholds]); k is chosen once and may not vary per shift or be selected after seeing results. The agreement and saving criteria are evaluated only on live-lineage edit cases (live_lineage_edit_cases >= 1; upstream segments inserted, shifted or reordered with every recorded segment unchanged, no segment in the page's lineage revoked), whose results are tagged Approximate; upstream-revocation cases are safety checks (revoked_upstream_page_reachable_count == 0, layers >= 1 freshly prefilled) and are excluded from the speed metric, and a run with live_lineage_edit_cases < 1 is vacuous and falsified. Same-position (zero-shift) reuse does not count toward the hypothesis; reuse must be exercised through the catalog and its position-independent secondary index (secondary_index_lookups >= 1), not pages handed to the backend, and must include non-page-aligned insertions of 1 token and 7 tokens (nonaligned_insertion_cases >= 1 for each) and a case where an upstream segment is revoked after the page was cached (upstream_revocation_cases >= 1): the page must be unreachable (revoked_upstream_page_reachable_count == 0) and layers >= 1 freshly prefilled (layers_ge1_freshly_prefilled >= 1, no layer >= 1 reused); any zero attempt counter makes the run vacuous and falsified; prefill_speedup = full_prefill_wall_time / reuse_prefill_wall_time (>= 2.0 required) and top1_agreement is the fraction of next-token positions whose argmax equals full prefill's; a per-candidate-k x per-shift results artifact candidate_shift_results.json must record top1_agreement and prefill_speedup for every k in [4, 7, 10, 13] and every shift in [16, 64, 256] (12 cells); any missing cell falsifies, and the existential single-k selection rule applies only over complete rows (a k with any missing cell is not eligible); k selection: k is chosen on a disjoint calibration split as the smallest k in [4, 7, 10, 13] with top1_agreement >= 99% at every shift and the criteria are then evaluated on the held-out split (this replaces any 'best k'); the tokens recomputed are the CacheBlend-style highest-deviation tokens in every layer >= 1; the 1-token and 7-token non-aligned insertions count toward the agreement and speedup criteria; prefill_speedup includes lookup, secondary-index, repack and recompute time and is the median after the pinned warm-up; reused_results_tagged_approximate must equal reused_results and approximate_tag_violations == 0; revoked_page_layers_reused == 0; a content edit that removes a canary (canary_removing_edit_cases >= 1) must recompute all layers with the canary not probeable (canary_probes >= 1, canary_logit_shift == 0); post_revocation_secondary_lookups_matching_content >= 1 with no hit on the revoked page; also an arm where the revocation is committed but invalidate_inputs (or the engine flush) is fault-injected to be dropped (invalidation_suppressed_arms >= 1), and an arm where the catalog is restored from a pre-revocation snapshot or tier object or the runtime restarts (restore_arms >= 1), each with post_event_lookups >= 1 and stale_hits == 0; every seed must meet every criterion.

## Rule
Record matched baselines, hardware, seeds and negative results. Do not change success criteria after observing results.
