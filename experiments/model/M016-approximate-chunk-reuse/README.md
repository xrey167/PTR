# M016-approximate-chunk-reuse

## Hypothesis
on pinned pretrained Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B (24 layers, N >= 2), position-independent chunk reuse at nonzero position shifts (pinned shifts 16, 64 and 256 tokens) with selective recompute (candidate k pinned as ceil(f * 64) tokens per 64-token chunk for f in [0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20], i.e. k in {4, 7, 10, 13}) keeps top-1 next-token agreement >= 99% while saving >= 2x prefill on a 512-token prompt with 7 of 8 chunks reused (448 reused tokens and a 64-token suffix); k_sel is the smallest k with calibration-split agreement >= 99% at every shift and is then evaluated on a held-out split; recomputed tokens are the CacheBlend-style highest-deviation tokens in every layer >= 1 and agreement is counted only at positions whose K/V used approximate rows; the 2x saving is measured only on the live-lineage insert and shift path (whole new segments inserted, or segments shifted or reordered, every recorded segment unchanged and nothing in the page's lineage revoked), whose results are tagged Approximate; content edits, including an insertion inside a segment, deletions and redactions, supersede the old generation (path a), so the page is unreachable for every layer and freshly prefilled with zero reuse; upstream-revocation cases are safety checks excluded from the speed metric; reuse stays correct under lagging or lost invalidation and restore or restart; admission additionally requires that every segment of the page's original prefix (identity, generation, digest) is present in the requesting prefix as a multiset (insertion, shift and reorder allowed, omission and deletion not); dependency invalidation (J0): an explicit revocation or a committed supersession, i.e. a generation replacement or edit, of any generation-bearing dependency; every protective mechanism of this experiment triggers on any invalidation, for the dependency kinds it covers (upstream segment inputs; the experiment is a single-shot oracle with no in-flight requests, so no in-flight race applies).

## Baseline
full prefill

## Falsification
The run is invalid (vacuous) and counts as falsified if any coverage prerequisite is unmet: live_lineage_insert_shift_cases < 1 or secondary_index_lookups < 1 (reuse must go through the catalog and its secondary index); nonaligned_insertion_cases < 1 for the 1-token insertion or for the 7-token insertion; upstream_revocation_cases < 1, in_segment_insertion_cases < 1, canary_removing_edit_cases < 1, canary_probes < 1 or post_revocation_secondary_lookups_matching_content < 1; candidate_shift_results.json has fewer than the 20 expected cells (4 values of k x (3 shifts + the 1-token and 7-token insertion cases)) with top1_agreement and prefill_speedup each (candidate_shift_results_cells < 20); a k with any missing cell is not eligible; invalidation_suppressed_arms < 1, restore_arms < 1, restored_pre_revocation_entries < 1 (the restored pre-revocation entry must be observed in the catalog before the post-event lookup), stale_eligible_entries_before_event < 1 or post_event_lookups_on_stale_eligible_entries != stale_eligible_entries_before_event (the arm where the revocation is committed but invalidate_inputs or the engine flush is fault-injected to be dropped and the arm where an actual pre-revocation entry (snapshot or tier object) is restored into the catalog must each be run, with post-event lookups targeting exactly the stale-eligible entries, post_event_lookups >= 1; a plain runtime restart is the separate reported arm empty_restart_arm and does not satisfy restore coverage); omitted_upstream_segment_cases < 1 (a request omitting a segment of the page's original prefix); upstream_supersession_cases < 1 (an upstream segment superseded, i.e. its generation replaced or edited, after the page was cached); selection_digest_mismatches > 0 (the corpus selection digest recorded in run.json differs from the pinned digest) or procedure_mismatches > 0 (a pinned run-procedure parameter recorded per arm or cell in run.json differs from the pinned value; equality across arms is also compared against the pinned value); timing_procedure_mismatches > 0 (warmup_runs or timed_runs recorded per cell differ from the pinned 3 and 11); fresh_prefills_completed_after_revocation < upstream_revocation_cases (every revoked-page case must complete via a fresh prefill). Falsified if any of: no k in [4, 7, 10, 13] reaches calibration-split top1_agreement >= 99% at every shift (k_sel does not exist); on the held-out split, k_sel has top1_agreement < 99% or median prefill_speedup < 2.0 at any pinned shift (the 1-token and 7-token insertion cells count); approximate_tag_violations > 0 or reused_results_tagged_approximate < reused_results; revoked_page_layers_reused > 0, revoked_upstream_page_reachable_count > 0, layers_ge1_freshly_prefilled below the number of affected layers, or any post-revocation secondary lookup returning the revoked page; in_segment_insertion_reused_tokens > 0; canary_logit_shift != 0 after the canary-removing content edit; stale_hits > 0 in the dropped-invalidation arm or the restore arm; omitted_upstream_admissions > 0 (a page admitted although a segment of its original prefix is absent from the requesting prefix); superseded_upstream_page_reachable_count > 0 (a page whose upstream segment generation was superseded still reachable, or any post-supersession secondary lookup returning it); fresh_prefill_output_mismatches > 0 (the fresh prefill output differs from a full prefill); any one of the 5 listed seeds misses any criterion above (every seed must pass). prefill_speedup = full_prefill_wall_time / reuse_prefill_wall_time including lookup, secondary-index, repack, deviation-selection and recompute time, as the median after the pinned warm-up. Analytic bound (config.toml): cost per layer-token units, full = 512 x 24 = 12288; reuse = 64 x 24 (suffix) + 448 x 1 (deviation selection over reused tokens) + 7 x k x 23 (recompute in layers >= 1); ideal speedup 4.68 (k=4), 3.95 (k=7), 3.42 (k=10), 3.01 (k=13), all >= 2 so no candidate is dropped.

## Rule
Record matched baselines, hardware, seeds and negative results. Do not change success criteria after observing results.
