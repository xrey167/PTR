# M013-expert-lifecycle

## Hypothesis
a gated expert lifecycle (birth, merge, split, freeze, prune, reactivate) under domain shift forgets less and matches a static expert set at equal training compute (FLOPs) and equal active parameters (final active parameters within 1% per seed), with average forgetting strictly below the static arm's and within the complete ForgettingGate configuration and public-suite quality and final per-domain and mean accuracy each within 1.0 point of static; reactivating a retired expert recovers a returning domain faster than a new expert, measured as steps_to_recovery to 95% of the expert's pre-retirement domain score. In a context-ablation arm, L3 structural proposals generated with full self-observation context (routing traces, load, churn, interference, AIMER scores) have a higher gate pass rate, by a pinned absolute margin, than both the same policy with that context removed and a fixed threshold-rule heuristic on load/overlap, and a mean net quality change per accepted proposal that is above a pinned minimum (> 0) and at least that of both baselines; training_flops includes discarded candidates, the L3 policy and gate evaluation, and the net quality metric is measured on a held-out domain suite disjoint from the gate suite over a pinned domain sequence; claim 11 is evaluated only if each lifecycle operator (Birth, Merge, Split, Freeze, Prune) has at least 2 accepted and gate-processed proposals in each lifecycle arm, otherwise the run is vacuous for claim 11.

## Primary metrics
average_forgetting, backward_transfer, accuracy_matrix, expert_count, steps_to_recovery, proposal_gate_pass_rate, net_quality_change_per_accepted_proposal, task_forgetting, public_suite_score, public_regression, gate_pass, proposals_generated, proposals_accepted, training_flops, active_parameters, lifecycle_operation_counts, final_domain_accuracy, final_mean_accuracy, static_arm_schedule_mismatches, static_arm_eval_stage_mismatches, accepted_proposals_by_operator, tuning_trials_used, tuning_budget_mismatches, paired_reactivation_trials, active_param_mismatch_seeds, selection_digest_mismatches, procedure_mismatches

## Baseline
static expert set at equal training compute and equal active parameters; naive adapter chain; context-ablated L3 policy (self-observation context removed); fixed heuristic baseline (threshold rules on load/overlap)

## Falsification
The run is invalid (vacuous) and counts as falsified if any coverage prerequisite is unmet: any lifecycle operation (birth, merge, split, freeze, prune, reactivate) has fewer than 1 exercise in lifecycle_operation_counts, or paired_reactivation_trials < 1 in any seed (claim 12 needs, per seed, at least one paired trial of reactivating a retired expert versus training a fresh new expert on the same returning domain; otherwise claim 12 is vacuous); proposals_generated below the pinned minimum (20) or proposals_accepted below the pinned minimum (10) in any of the proposal-generating arms (the full-context arm, the context-ablated arm and the heuristic arm; the static arm and the naive adapter chain do not generate proposals); the lifecycle and static arms differ in training_flops by more than flops_relative_tolerance, or active_param_mismatch_seeds > 0 (in any seed the lifecycle arm's final active_parameters differ from the static arm's by more than active_params_relative_tolerance = 0.01; both declared per arm); static_arm_schedule_mismatches > 0 or static_arm_eval_stage_mismatches > 0 (the static arm must be observed matched to the lifecycle arm: the same domain-shift schedule and the same evaluation stages, with training_flops within flops_relative_tolerance, instead of a proposal minimum); accepted_proposals_by_operator < 2 for any of Birth, Merge, Split, Freeze or Prune in any lifecycle arm (the full-context, context-ablated and heuristic arms; vacuous for claim 11); tuning_budget_mismatches > 0 (tuning_trials_used per arm, for the lifecycle, static, context-ablated and heuristic arms, differs from the pinned 8); selection_digest_mismatches > 0 (the corpus selection digest recorded in run.json differs from the pinned digest) or procedure_mismatches > 0 (a pinned run-procedure parameter recorded per arm or cell in run.json differs from the pinned value; equality across arms is also compared against the pinned value). Falsified if any of: claim 11: average_forgetting of the lifecycle arm not strictly below the matched static arm's; claim 11: the ForgettingGate (gate_pass, GateReport) fails on any of average_forgetting > gate_max_average_forgetting, any task's forgetting > gate_max_task_forgetting, backward_transfer < gate_min_backward_transfer, or public_regression (serving public score minus candidate public score) > gate_max_public_regression (equality passes each threshold); claim 11: public-suite quality, or final per-domain accuracy, or final mean accuracy (final_domain_accuracy, final_mean_accuracy) of the lifecycle arm more than 1.0 point below the static arm's; claim 12: reactivation of a retired expert does not reach 95% of the expert's pre-retirement domain score in fewer steps than birth of a new expert (steps_to_recovery capped at max_recovery_steps; a new expert that does not reach the target is right-censored at the cap, a reactivated expert that does not reach it within the cap falsifies); claim 13: the full-context arm beats the context-ablated arm on proposal_gate_pass_rate by less than 0.05 absolute, or (independently) beats the heuristic arm by less than 0.05 absolute; claim 13: mean net_quality_change_per_accepted_proposal of the full-context policy not > 0.0, or less than baseline + net_quality_vs_baseline_margin (0.0) for either baseline; any one of the 5 listed seeds misses any criterion above (every seed must pass). training_flops includes discarded candidates, the L3 policy and gate evaluation; the net quality metric is the held-out domain-suite accuracy delta over the pinned domain sequence, disjoint from the gate suite.

## Rule
Record matched baselines, hardware, seeds and negative results. Do not change success criteria after observing results.
