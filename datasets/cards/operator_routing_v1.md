# Dataset Card: operator_routing_v1

- Version: v1 (`generator_version = 1`, benchmark seed 20260925, codebook version 1, fingerprint `2b6f8175a7a7bb648910e7acb17dcfcff5149834f2fe87355c5d2c4524c113a3`)
- Source / generator: Fully synthetic. `benchmarks/operator-routing/generator.py` (Python standard library only) draws every example from its own splitmix64 stream and labels it with a fixed rule; `benchmarks/operator-routing/README.md` is the specification
- License / rights: Project-generated by PTR; no third-party content
- Sensitive data: None. Entities are integer ids and the English `task` text is rendered from the generated fields
- Schema: `datasets/schemas/operator_route.schema.json` (JSONL, one record per example, operator names from the codebook) plus a TSV twin (`id, label_code, R, B, tokens, facts`) that the Rust study binary reads; one record per split is committed as `datasets/samples/operator_route_v1.jsonl`
- Size: 36,000 examples: train 16,000, val 2,000, test_iid 3,000, and five OOD test splits of 3,000 (ood_compose_epi, ood_compose_regime, ood_distractors, ood_validity, ood_payload); about 91 MB of JSONL and 9 MB of TSV, regenerated in about 12 s
- Split strategy: Every split is generated from its own per-example streams (split name and index). The OOD splits each change one factor relative to train: held-out (role, epistemic) pairs, the (Evidence, interventional) cell, 4.6x filler tokens, p_live 0.50, and an unseen entity-id pool
- Contamination controls: The 9 held-out (role, epistemic) pairs never occur outside ood_compose_epi, and Evidence never occurs under the interventional regime outside ood_compose_regime; both are asserted by `benchmarks/operator-routing/tests/`. The ood_payload entity pool (256..511) is disjoint from every other split's (0..255)
- Intended training stages: The A0 mechanism study (research/falsification/A0-ablations-v1): train for training, val for pre-freeze calibration, the learning-rate sweep and learning curves
- Evaluation exclusions: test_iid and the five OOD splits are evaluation-only; they must never be trained on or used to select hyperparameters
- Known limitations: Synthetic, with an invented rule, costs and affinities designed by the same people who designed A0 (the rule has the form of A0's admitted-mean router). Index-tagged raw tokens make binding much easier than natural text. One benchmark seed; results say nothing about language, real routing costs or other data draws
- SHA-256 / manifest: The generated files are git-ignored; their SHA-256 values, the data FNV-1a-64 (`0ad71688f09b0d0d`) and per-split label FNV-1a-64 values are pinned in `benchmarks/operator-routing/splits.lock.json`, and `generator.py --check` verifies them
