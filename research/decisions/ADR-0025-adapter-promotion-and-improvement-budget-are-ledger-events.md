# ADR-0025 — Adapter Promotion and the Improvement Budget Are Ledger Events

## Status
Accepted for the prototype with research-integration work package 5 (`ptr-lineage`, `ptr-ledger`, `ptr-runtime`, `ptr-feedback`, `ptr-model-api`). It builds on ADR-0019 (adapter lineage), ADR-0009 (ledger is authority) and ADR-0018 (revocable derived state), and closes the open item that "no registry row decides which adapter serves" left standing.

## Decision
1. **Serving is a ledger fact.** `AdapterPromoted` (tag 39) and `AdapterRevoked` (tag 40) are the only records that make an adapter generation serve or stop serving. They are written only by `PtrRuntime::promote_adapter` and `revoke_adapter`; the public `commit` refuses them. The runtime derives the serving state from the committed events under the project-scoped lifecycle key `ptr-adapter:<project>/<adapter>`, and every use is checked against it (`check_adapter_use`), so a revocation holds against caches, restarts and replay (invariant 3).
2. **A promotion needs a baseline.** The gate reports `GateViolation::MissingBaseline` when the serving score of the public suite is absent, and a report with that violation cannot become a `PromotionProposal`. A missing baseline is never read as zero.
3. **The runtime recomputes the weights digest.** `promote_adapter` hashes the weights it is handed and compares with the digest the gate report was bound to; it also compares the frozen split manifest with the report's evaluation manifest digest. The lineage crate keeps recording the trainer's digest without reading it and takes no dependency on the ledger.
4. **The improvement budget is counted from the ledger.** `ImprovementCycleRecorded` (tag 41) records every cycle that ended, completed or aborted, with the day and the policy digest. The rule lives in `ptr-feedback` (`ImprovementCycleBudget`); the runtime supplies the history from committed events plus the cycles admitted and not yet recorded. An aborted cycle and a running cycle spend budget. With no budget or clock configured nothing is admitted. The day is read from a runtime `CycleClock`, not given by the caller, and a project's high-water day (admitted or recorded) refuses any earlier day.
5. **No simulator before an out-of-sample prediction.** The replay backtest reports `pipeline_ran` and `predictive` as separate claims, `predictive` needing a pinned minimum count and share of generations predicted within a pinned error bound, flags `degenerate`, `not_identified` and `extrapolation`, and no winner is chosen from a history that predicted nothing or that extrapolates. No simulator training is built in PTR until `predictive` has been true on a real generation history at least once.
6. **Batching sits behind the backend traits.** `AdaptiveBatcher` and `ReplicaRouter` implement `InferenceBackend`/`ResumableInferenceBackend`, are bounded, drop passed deadlines before the model, and ask a gate about the batch's generation immediately before every model call. They are not wired into `ptrd` until the server stops holding its lock across the model call.

## Consequences
- The three events take tags 39, 40 and 41 (37 is `EgressRefused`, 38 `PodHeadAdvanced`; 32 to 36 are reserved by ADR-0022). Each is added at the end of every exhaustive match, in tag order.
- Adapter bindings and cycle counts are not part of any compacted snapshot layout. `export_compacted_snapshot` refuses while they exist; a snapshot section is a follow-up. This trades a missing feature for never dropping a revocation silently.
- A cycle admitted and not recorded before a crash is not counted after the restart. The budget bounds recorded and running cycles.
- The weights digest proves what the caller passed hashes to what the gate saw; it does not prove the bytes are the stored artifact, and the gate subject is the trusted evaluator's claim.
- A promotion is justified only by a report that `ForgettingGate::evaluate_promotion` produced in this process; the plain `evaluate` path (where a placeholder serving score is possible) and a decoded report cannot become a `PromotionProposal`. The project is bound into the digested subject through the operation id.
- Evidence of the batcher's value does not exist: the default `infer_batch` is sequential, the server lock hides any gain, and the experiment that would measure it, E014 (planned, no entrypoint), cannot be frozen until the server-lock restructuring. Until it runs, the batcher is a bounded, tested mechanism and not a performance claim.
- The mechanisms are covered by tests; the claims about the backtest and about adapter lineage are registered, planned experiments (F008 and R004; E014 for batching). None of them is enrolled in `experiments/preregistration.toml` yet, none has a run, and they are not evidence.
