# A0 ablation study v2: post-run audit (hand-written, not generated)

An independent read-only audit recomputed every per-seed delta, mean, SD, 95% t-interval (t = 2.776, n = 5) and positive-seed count from the raw `correct/n` values in `metrics/*.json` and found them equal to `results.json` and `RESULTS.md`. Every verdict follows `criteria.toml` mechanically. No code, script, test, model or CI file changed between the evaluation tag `a0-ablations-v2-eval` (`94d9a9e0`) and the final aggregate. The points below are disclosures and framing corrections, not changes to any verdict. `RESULTS.md` is generated by `scripts/report_a0_ablation.py`; if it is ever regenerated, its hand-added section must be restored from this file.

## 1. M001-secondary at S* (undisclosed in the generated report)

At S* (1500 steps) full minus no-semantic-slots on composite A was +0.2009, 95% interval [+0.1197, +0.2821], 5/5 seeds positive. It could not be issued as SUPPORTS because no-semantic-slots missed its learnability bar (0.6408 < 0.6510). The frozen rule therefore moved the decision to 4000 steps (`scripts/aggregate_a0_ablation.py`, unchanged since the evaluation tag), where the gap is +0.0702, interval [-0.0936, +0.2340], 3/5 seeds positive. Seed 17 (no-semantic-slots at 0.526 test_iid) carries nearly all of that mean; the other four seeds average +0.0118. The S* value is not the deciding evidence and is reported only so the reader sees what the rule replaced. M001-primary (1500 steps) and M001-secondary (4000 steps) are decided at different budgets and are not comparable.

## 2. Contingency registration: a deviation from the governing text

`PREREGISTRATION.md` line 45 says contingency experiments are registered at the second freeze, before any evaluation seed, and its line 7 says it governs over `DESIGN.md` where the two differ. `DESIGN.md` D4 and procedure step 7 say a contingency is registered in a later commit, only if the learnability rule triggers. The run followed D4: `M001-a0v2-cont-no-semantic-slots` was registered in `49fd055c`, after the evaluation aggregate (`fc38fbea`) had shown the trigger. This is a deviation from the governing text. What did not change after outcomes were known: `criteria.toml`, the aggregator's contingency rule (present at the evaluation tag), the arms (full, no-semantic-slots), the seeds (the five evaluation seeds), the step budget (4000, from `criteria.toml`) and the selected learning rates. No discretionary choice was available. The frozen files were not edited. The preliminary aggregate `fc38fbea` was withdrawn in `1f4d36a4` before the contingency ran, because the code-identity rule counts files outside run records, logs and later-stage registration as code; it remains in history. Lesson for successor studies: register contingency experiments for every arm at the second freeze (as the preregistration text says), or do not commit aggregate outputs before a contingency.

## 3. M002-necessity: a weak exclusion

The FALSIFIES verdict rests on an interval upper bound of +0.0160 against a 0.02 margin, from five seeds with 3/5 positive (range -1.9 to +1.5 points). Half of composite B is `ood_compose_regime`, on which no arm scores above 0.47; on the other half (`ood_distractors`) full minus no-typed-attention is +0.0041. The M004 negative control found a difference of about 3 points between two models equivalent up to reparameterisation. Read the verdict as "candidate for simplification in A0", not "shown useless". M002-sufficiency (+0.1026, 5/5) shows the typed bias does learn role-gated reading when it is the only metadata-dependent read.

## 4. Further reading notes

- M004-negative-control "SUPPORTS" is the rule's label for a failed equivalence: its interval [+0.0203, +0.0452] lies outside ±0.02, so the negative control did not come out equivalent. It is not evidence for routing.
- M001-primary is INCONCLUSIVE only through the 5/5 clause (seed 29, where the masked arm trained to 0.881). The mean gap is +0.19 with lower bound +0.046, so this is not evidence of no effect either. The masked arm passes its bar by 0.012 and is bimodal across seeds (0.503-0.881).
- M003-depth narrowly misses the minimum effect (mean +0.0190 against 0.02; upper bound +0.0339).
- The process sentences in `RESULTS.md` ("its record was committed alone before the next launch") are fixed report text; `git log` confirms they hold for this run (one commit per record).
- The 4000-step figures quoted in `RESULTS.md` line 127 come from the report script's `later_metrics`, not from `results.json`; they were not independently recomputed.
- The v1 files (`../A0-ablations-v1/`) are frozen and were not edited. Per `DESIGN.md` ("Which v1 verdicts v2 can supersede"), with every v2 gate passing, these v2 verdicts supersede v1's INCONCLUSIVE results for the same contrasts.
