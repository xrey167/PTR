## After the freeze: what happened, in order

- The lr-selection commit (4b0ad16) held only `lr_selection.*` and the sweep records. The evaluation, the rerun and the contingency all ran from it with a clean worktree: gate G4 checks the evaluation records, and the rerun and contingency records show the same commit and `git_dirty = false`.
- `no-semantic-slots` fell below its learnability bar at S* (0.6307 < 0.6510), which triggered the preregistered contingency: that arm and `full` re-run at 4000 steps with the same seeds and learning rates. M001-secondary is decided on those runs. There the arm cleared the bar (0.8685 >= 0.6510).
- The aggregator as frozen did not re-check the learnability bar on the 4000-step runs, which the criteria require (an arm still below it makes the contrast INCONCLUSIVE). It was fixed in commit bd84b48 after the contingency runs finished and before any of their results were read, with a test that fails against the frozen code; the criteria did not change.
- A mistake in process, recorded for completeness: while the contingency's first three processes were running, the fix was briefly present as an uncommitted edit, which would have marked any process started then as dirty. It was stashed before the remaining two processes started; all five contingency records show `git_dirty = false` at 4b0ad16.
- The preregistration tag `a0-ablation-prereg-v1` points at 45b2d54. It exists in the working copy the study ran in; this session could push the branch but not tags, so recreate it with `git tag a0-ablation-prereg-v1 45b2d54` before re-running the aggregator elsewhere.

## Interpretation (not a rule output)

Typed slot content helped at the preregistered budget: on the OOD composite A the full arm beat the masked content-free arm by +0.1183 on average at 1500 steps. The contingency qualifies that. Given 4000 steps, the unmasked content-free arm, which must bind every fact from index-tagged raw tokens and learn the Live gate itself, rose from 0.5195 to 0.7178 on composite A, while the full arm stayed at 0.7311 and 0.7307; the gap closed to +0.0129 (interval [-0.0262, +0.0520]). At 1500 steps the masked arm scored 0.6128 there; it was not re-run at 4000 steps, so M001-primary's margin is known only at 1500. Much of what typed slots buy in this task may therefore be learning speed rather than a capability the raw path lacks; a study at matched longer budgets for every arm would separate the two.

For the typed attention bias and for a second tied refinement step, a benefit of 2 points or more is excluded at this scale, as the design expected for the first; both are candidates for simplification. The per-slot nonlinearity, by contrast, is used: removing the latent step costs 8.0 points on test_iid, and removing only its gelu costs 6.5. The frozen router could not be shown equivalent to the learned one within the preregistered band, and nothing about routing follows from it either way.

## Reading these results

Every verdict holds only for this A0 (one block, d_model 48, 1500 steps) on operator-routing v1, a synthetic task whose label rule was written to have the same form as A0's admitted-mean router. Nothing here is evidence about language-model quality, and nothing here is the M001-M004 baseline comparison, which needs the plain model baseline (owner decision O2). `PREREGISTRATION.md` lists the deviations from the design and what the study cannot show.
