study and are not edited after the freeze. Whatever happens to the study after it, and was not
foreseen by that text, is recorded here, in order, append-only: what happened, what was and was
not observed, what was done, and what the final `RESULTS.md` must say. This file changes no
criterion, threshold, protocol value, seed or rule and is not part of any digest pin.

`../A0-baseline-v2/DEVIATIONS.md` ("Procedure: an interrupted run of a listed experiment"); it
applies here unchanged. The story of why this study exists is in `DESIGN.md` and in D1 and D3 of
the v2 deviations; it is not a deviation of this study.
