# Research agenda

[`MOE_RESEARCH_AGENDA.md`](MOE_RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) is a proposal (2026-10-13), not evidence. It turns PTR's mixture-of-experts ideas into at most three falsifiable theses. It contains an inventory of every MoE-relevant claim, mechanism and experiment with its evidence class: the repository holds no research result about any MoE model yet. It also contains a 2024–2026 literature check with arXiv ids, which marks verifier-conditioned routing as done and lifecycle and revocable experts as partially done. The three theses are failure-mode experts, off-policy-auditable routing, and lineage-closed revocation of experts. Each comes with its baseline, ablations, pinned thresholds, kill criteria and compute estimate. The document ends with work packages, risks, and proposed edits to `research/novelty/`, `experiments/registry.toml` and the ADRs, none of which it makes itself.
